# ADR-0070: Math v2 — TeX classes, ink geometry, style levels

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Related: ADR-0026 (display math), ADR-0027 (inline math), ADR-0030 (math
  completion), ADR-0054/0056 (italic variables, tags), ADR-0069 (default
  template); proposal `docs/proposal-math-typography.md`; D71, D93
- Touches: `soma-core` (`MathParam`, three metrics hooks), `soma-metrics`
  (`soma.metrics/5`), `tools/metrics-gen` (MATH table, glyph ink), `soma-math`
  (classes, spacing, style, geometry, segments), `soma-layout` (math adapter,
  inline split, display skips), `soma-rules` (math break boundary), the sample
  scripts' math face

## Context

Math could be parsed and drawn, but not typeset: an audit of the sample and of
`soma-math` found three root causes rather than a list of missing symbols.

1. **Spacing only knew Bin and Rel.** `Class` had six variants and `gap()`
   returned zero for everything else. `\sin x` was set as `sinx` (`\sin` was an
   `Ord`), a large operator had no thin space before its operand, a comma was
   not followed by a thin space, and a Bin after a Rel kept its medium space.
   `\!` was parsed as `Space(0)` while the documentation promised −167.
2. **Vertical geometry used the em box, not the glyph's ink.** `MathMetrics`
   exposed only a face-wide ascent/descent, so a radical bar sat on the font's
   ascender, `\left(…\right)` was sized to the body's em box, and fractions
   lifted a numerator by the font's descent. The final box height was inflated
   the same way, which also moved every page that contained a formula.
3. **Style was a size multiplier.** `scriptscript_scale` was never read, so
   every nested script shrank by 0.7 again; there was no cramped style, no
   TeX rule 18 for superscript/subscript placement, and no italic correction.

## Decision

### 1. Math data lives in the metrics table

`soma.metrics/5` adds, per face:

```text
u16 math_constant_count
per constant: i32 value (per-mille of one em)
u32 math_glyph_count
per glyph: varint(codepoint delta) | i16 top | i16 bottom | i16 italic
```

`soma_core::MathParam` fixes the constant order (35 values, named after the
OpenType `MATH` fields: axis height, rule thickness, script percentages,
superscript/subscript shifts and drops, fraction numerator/denominator shifts
and gaps, limit gaps, radical constants, overbar/underbar, accent base height).
`metrics-gen` reads the font's `MATH` table and converts font units to
per-mille (`percent × 10` for the two percentages). Per-glyph `top`/`bottom`
come from the `glyf` bounding box; `italic` comes from
`MathGlyphInfo/MathItalicsCorrectionInfo` (coverage table, format 1 or 2).
A face without `MATH` or without `glyf` writes zero counts, and `soma-math`
keeps its TeX fallback values.

The kernel gains three defaulted hooks on `Metrics`: `math_param`, `math_ink`,
`math_italic`. Both adapters (`TableMetrics`, `StyledMetrics`) answer them from
the math face. A CFF math face therefore gets constants but no ink; the em box
is the fallback.

### 2. Eight atom classes and TeX's spacing table

`Class` is now `Ord Op Bin Rel Open Close Punct Inner`, and `gap()` implements
the TeXbook chapter 18 table (0 / thin 3mu / medium 4mu / thick 5mu; `*`
entries fall back to Ord). Medium and thick spaces are suppressed in script
styles (`\nonscript`). A Bin at a list edge, or after Bin/Op/Rel/Open/Punct,
becomes Ord; a Bin before Rel/Close/Punct does too. Function names are `Op`;
`\sum` and friends are `Op`; fractions, binomials and `\left…\right` are
`Inner`. `\!` is a negative thin space, `\:` a medium one.

### 3. Four style levels, cramped, rule 18

`StyleLevel` is `Display/Text/Script/ScriptScript`. Script sizes are
percentages of the **formula's base size**, so a script inside a script does
not shrink again; a fraction sets its numerator/denominator in the next style
down (display → text, text → script), with the denominator cramped.
Superscripts use `sup_shift`/`sup_shift_cramped`, are raised at least
`base_height − sup_drop` (so a tall base does not collide), and keep
`sup_bottom_min` above the baseline; subscripts drop by
`max(sub_shift, base_depth + sub_drop)`; when both exist the
`sub_sup_gap`/`sup_bottom_max_sub` rules apply. A superscript after a single
italic glyph starts after the glyph's italic correction, and a scripted group
ends with `\scriptspace`. `\displaystyle` and friends switch the rest of the
group.

### 4. Ink-based box geometry

- **Fractions**: numerator/denominator baselines use the font's shifts
  (`num1`/`num2`, `denom1`/`denom2`) with `num_gap`/`denom_gap` as the minimum
  clearance from the ink; the rule is `fractionRuleThickness`.
- **Radicals**: the vinculum sits on the radicand's ink top; the surd is scaled
  until its ink covers the body (up to four times the base size); the degree is
  placed with the radical kerns.
- **Big operators**: display size is at least `displayOperatorMinHeight`;
  limits use `upperLimitGapMin`/`upperLimitBaselineRiseMin` and the lower pair;
  integrals keep side scripts.
- **Delimiters**: selected by the body's ink height (still continuous scaling;
  font size variants are D93).
- **New nodes**: `\hat \widehat \bar \vec \tilde`, `\overline \underline`,
  `\operatorname`/`\operatorname*`, `\stackrel`, `\binom`, `\genfrac`,
  `\dfrac`/`\tfrac`/`\cfrac`, `\middle`, the eight `\math*` class commands,
  `\limits`/`\nolimits`, and `\:`/`\!`/`\thinspace`/`\negthinspace`.

### 5. Display skips and row leading

A math block takes its before/after glue from the template. The built-in
LaTeX template sets the 10 pt values (1em, plus 0.2em, minus 0.5em). When the
preceding line is short (it ends more than two ems before the measure), the
block uses TeX's short form: no space above and 70% of the space below. Matrix
and `aligned` rows use `\jot` (0.3em); `\substack` lines do too.

### 6. Inline math may break at relations and binaries

A formula wider than half the measure is split at the boundaries **before** a
Rel/Bin (after Bin normalisation). Each piece becomes an object atom; the
boundary between pieces carries the math spacing as glue and
`\relpenalty = 500` / `\binoppenalty = 700`. A broken line therefore starts
with the operator, exactly as TeX does. Short formulas stay one atom, so the
common case is unchanged. The page result keeps its shape: `inline_math` now
has one entry per piece, all with the same `source` and distinct atom indices.

### 7. Math face policy

The sample scripts and `scripts/fonts.sh` prefer a serif **TrueType** math face
(DejaVu Math TeX Gyre), because it matches a Chinese serif body and, having
`glyf`, gives the generator per-glyph ink. `Libertinus Math` is the second
choice; `Noto Sans Math` remains a last resort. A CFF math face still supplies
the constants.

## Consequences

- `soma.metrics` is version 5; `metricsVersion` is 5 and `metricsHash` changes.
  Older tables parse and keep TeX fallback geometry.
- Page results change wherever a formula appears: box heights and depths are
  ink-based, line breaks see the new object widths, and display skips are
  larger. The SVG Gate V hash and the sample page count move.
- Canonical page shape is unchanged (`soma.pages/10`).
- The default template's `mathBlock` glue changed; an explicit template still
  wins.
- `docs/math.md`, `docs/testing.md`, `examples/book/brief.tex` and the scripts
  move with the implementation.

## Deliberately not done

- Font size variants and assemblies for delimiters (D93). Scaling is
  continuous; a 3-em parenthesis is a scaled glyph, not a designed variant.
- MATH top-accent attachment and math kern (italic correction is the only
  glyph adjustment read).
- `\genfrac`'s style argument is accepted and ignored; `\intertext` and
  `alignat` remain unsupported (D71).
- Math inside `\text{}` keeps the body face and the em-box fallback.

## Alternatives

- **Keep the em box and only tune constants.** Rejected: the radical bar and
  the fences are visibly wrong by half an em, which no constant can fix.
- **Use a shaping library.** Rejected: math is a box model, not shaping, and
  the crate is `no_std` with no font access.
- **Put the MATH constants in the template.** Rejected: they belong to the
  face; a template must not need 35 numbers to set a formula.
